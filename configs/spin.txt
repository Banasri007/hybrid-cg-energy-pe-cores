# Spin test: does waiting-while-spinning explain why pure MPI costs ~1.6x energy on mixed P+E?
# MPI ranks spin in MPI_Allreduce by default; libgomp threads spin briefly then sleep.
# If OpenMP-only forced to spin (OMP_WAIT_POLICY=active) climbs to the MPI-only energy, spinning is the cause.
# mpi_yield_when_idle makes waiting ranks call sched_yield (still busy if nothing else is runnable).
A12 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15 12 1 static
A12-yield 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15 12 1 static - --mca,mpi_yield_when_idle,1
A12 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15 1 12 static
A12-ompactive 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15 1 12 static OMP_WAIT_POLICY=active
A12-omppassive 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15 1 12 static OMP_WAIT_POLICY=passive
M8 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11 8 1 static
M8-yield 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11 8 1 static - --mca,mpi_yield_when_idle,1
M8 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11 1 8 static
M8-ompactive 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11 1 8 static OMP_WAIT_POLICY=active
M8-omppassive 0,2,4,6,8,9,10,11 1 8 static OMP_WAIT_POLICY=passive
# control: homogeneous P-cores (no slow ranks to wait for)
P4 0,2,4,6 4 1 static
P4 0,2,4,6 1 4 static
P4-ompactive 0,2,4,6 1 4 static OMP_WAIT_POLICY=active
